## User request (verbatim, 2026-11-04)

> improve the heuristic of the janitor secret shield mod, so it will catch every possible secret knwon to you.

## Why

On 2026-11-03 a pm2 process dump put two PyPI tokens and a Dropbox app secret into a session. jr-redact (ten fixed formats, no key-name rule) hid only the OpenAI and Gemini keys. hooks/secret-shield/patterns.ts has token rules, a key-name assignment rule (SECRET_NAME / looksSecret) and URL passwords. The measurement below shows it does not cover every shape of that incident. This card closes that gap and widens the shield to every format and context in the catalog.

## Measured on 2026-11-04 (current code, run in bun)

- Plain JSON in the pm2 jlist shape, env lines and YAML: every fake value was removed. The PyPI tokens and the Dropbox secret were caught only by the key-name rule; the OpenAI key was caught by its own token rule.
- JSON nested in a string: the two PyPI fakes (built without the real pypi-AgE prefix, so no token rule applied) and the 15-character Dropbox secret were not removed by the key-name rule; with real-prefix fakes the pypi-token rule catches PyPI in every variant (incident test, 2026-11-04). This was the confirmed gap.
- Fixed in the escaped-JSON change: the key-name rule hides secret-named pairs in plain JSON, in JSON escaped at any depth, and in a value cut at the end of a line, also when the cut falls inside the closing escape. Still missed, for the context-rules step: single-quoted keys (Python dict reprs), unquoted keys (one-line Node util.inspect, JSON5), quotes written as a Unicode escape (a backslash, the letter u, then 0022), an escape sequence inside an escaped value, and HTML-entity or URL-encoded JSON.
- The PyPI token rule matches only a value with the real pypi-AgE prefix.
- Mods run in Bun, on JavaScriptCore. A pattern with a leading `(?i)` throws there; the scoped `(?i:...)` group, lookbehind, and `\p{L}` with the u flag all compile.
- Speed: 5 MB takes about 130 ms today, and an estimated 250 to 400 ms with about 200 rules. The hook limit is 10 s, so a prefilter is optional, and it may gate only prefix rules, never context rules.
- gitleaks and trufflehog are installed locally.
- These figures come from a simplified env object, from local bun 1.3.14 rather than the 1.4.4 inside Claude Code, and from clean input only; the raw pm2 shape and adversarial input were not measured.

## Acceptance criterion

Done means the shield removes every format in this card's catalog and every context rule in it, each proven by its own test. Known misses are listed in hooks/secret-shield/KNOWN-MISSES.md, tracked. This card never claims to catch every possible secret.
- The gap analysis against hooks/secret-shield/rules is written down and covers the whole corpus. Each secret-category key of the 2026-11-04 rebuilt catalog (108 repos) and of the 802 keys found only in the pre-removal build, each of the 31 regex keys of anubis CANDIDATE_PATTERNS.yaml, each needs-reconstruction row (278 in the rebuild, plus the 20 of the removed repos in the pre-removal build), each of the 5 keys invalid in every engine, each of the 36 keys the independent verifier could not verify, and each detection heuristic of the TRDD-IE8U6YRA ideas digest is listed as adopted, already covered, or rejected with a reason. The catalog's other categories are listed category by category, each judged out of scope or brought in with a reason. Prevention and remediation ideas in the digest are not detection rules and are out of this criterion. Every adopted rule compiles under JavaScriptCore and complies with the license policy decided 2026-11-04 (word for word only from MIT, Apache or BSD sources with NOTICE credit; otherwise re-derived). A key's verdict may be inherited from its format cluster, for the variants tested, when a direct test decided that cluster and the key's membership is verified (its own regex accepts the cluster's direct-test sample, or its samples, spot-checked, are hidden by that family's specific kind); membership by label or provider name alone is not enough, and the inheritance and its evidence are recorded per key. (Added 2026-11-04: per-key classification on synthetic samples was measured unreliable.)
- A source in a Tier A removed repo (removed.txt column 2 = A) never counts as the license basis for a word-for-word copy or a NOTICE credit, whatever other sources the key has; a key whose only permissive source is Tier A is re-derived from the provider's documentation.
- ReDoS check: every rule, shipped and adopted, run in Bun on inputs that end in a failing tail at 32 KB and 256 KB, stays under 100 ms at 256 KB and either grows no worse than linearly or stays under 2 ms at 256 KB, each key timed while the one-minute load average is below the core count, with the load average recorded; a key timed above it is inconclusive (neither adopted nor rejected) and is re-queued, and a slow or timed-out result is re-run under the same condition before the rule is rejected. The linear tolerance is the ratio 12 that jsc_compile.ts uses. (Changed 2026-11-04 from 10 ms: a quadratic rule at 9 ms at 256 KB takes about 3.6 s on a 5 MB result.)
- Known-secret propagation: after all rules have run, every other copy of each hidden value that propagates() admits (at least 10 characters and secret-shaped; not a name, path, slug, email, host:port or marker text) is hidden within the same redact(), redactBlocks() or redactDeep() call, in its raw, JSON-escaped and URL-encoded forms, proven by tests that put a copy outside any rule's match. Known misses, listed in KNOWN-MISSES.md: copies in separate calls or across a session, copies split by a line break, truncated copies, base64 forms, the JSON-decoded form of an escaped JSON value, a value overlapping another found value, the credential shapes propagates() knowingly refuses, strings nested deeper than 12 levels in a structured value, and blocks other than text blocks and tool_result content.

## Incident test

A test replays the 2026-11-03 incident with fake values in three shapes:

- raw single-line pm2 JSON that carries both env and pm2_env.env;
- the same JSON with a truncated last value that has no closing quote;
- an escaped-JSON variant, where the JSON is nested inside a string.

It asserts that each fake value is absent from the output. looksSecret is loosened for short values, such as the 15-character Dropbox secret, only when the key name is a secret name.

## Split of patterns.ts

The rules move into files under rules/ and are assembled into one explicitly ordered rule list, never ordered by import or file name. A precedence test checks that an `sk-ant-` key is reported with the Anthropic kind and that the private-key rule comes first. NOTICE records the new files as janitor-original. The test-assembly step copies rules/ as well.

## Self-scan gate

A test runs the redactor over this repo's tracked sources, its tests and the Python scanner tests. Each seeded hit is classified by review as a fake fixture or a false positive. Fake fixtures go into a frozen expected list, false positives fail and are fixed, and any new hit fails the test. An adversarial timing test feeds `password=` followed by 1 MB of one repeated character with no terminator, and a long URL with no `@`, and asserts that each finishes inside a runner timeout well under the 10 s hook limit, because a backtracking regex hangs instead of failing.

## gitleaks check

When gitleaks is installed, a test writes gitleaks' own positive examples, or samples generated from its rules, to a temp directory. It runs gitleaks over them and over the false-positive corpus, writes its report to reports_dev/, and fails on any gitleaks positive the shield misses that is not in KNOWN-MISSES.md.

Porting a gitleaks rule rewrites its leading `(?i)` into the scoped `(?i:...)` form. It also translates RE2-only syntax, such as POSIX classes, `(?P<name>...)`, `\z` and `\Q...\E`, and any entropy filter. A rule that still does not compile, or whose filter cannot be kept, is listed in KNOWN-MISSES.md and never silently dropped. CI installs gitleaks, and anywhere it is missing the test reports a skip as a coverage gap, never as a pass. gitleaks keeps its positive examples in Go source, so the samples are extracted from there or generated from each rule's regex.
