# ledger-to-card-detector


^ATOM-3VFM-DCAJ [desc: "ledger-to-card.py turns recurring findings into tickets or proposal cards (LEDGER-001 to 004), capped at 5 raises per fire and 3 proposals per day", keywords: recurring_findings_never_acted_on ledger-to-card.py_hourly_detector LEDGER-001_LEDGER-002_LEDGER-003_LEDGER-004 PRRD_G34.1_every_issue_becomes_a_ticket_or_a_TRDD proposal_card_from_a_finding_design/proposals needs_human_ticket_never_dispatched ledger_group_code_src_recurs_3_entries_or_2_an_hour_apart dedupe_key_LEDGER_code_src_no_date caps_5_raises_per_fire_3_proposals_per_day no_severity_floor_93_percent_of_ledger_LOW issue_codes_gen.py_build_issue_codes.py_ISSUE-CODES.md ticket_proposal.py_cards_for_key refused_proposal_stays_refused_evidence_code_src, type: reference, trdd: TRDD-IP23RNLY, ocd: 2026-11-19, lmd: 2026-11-19]

Owner, 2026-11-19, verbatim (PRRD G34.1): "issues must be solved not just reported. every issue must become either a janitor ticket or a TRDD. the janitor must ensure that, enforce that, nudge that!" Nothing read the findings ledger and filed work from it, and most entries were LOW advisories recorded and never acted on. d8c93ad3 (TRDD-IP23RNLY) added the hourly detector scripts/detectors/ledger-to-card.py (not quiet-filtered, so it reaches the session; registered in scripts/dispatch.py). It groups ledger entries (scripts/lib/findings_ledger.py entries()) by (code, src) and, for a group that recurs (3 or more entries, or 2 at least 1 h apart) and has no open card, calls issue_catalog.raise_issue: LEDGER-001 janitor machinery (the src table is in the code): a HARNESS ticket, auto-dispatched; LEDGER-002 project repo or board: a proposal TRDD in design/proposals with an approval command (scripts/lib/ticket_proposal.py, cards_for_key); LEDGER-003 owner-only work (an iTerm TCC permission, a stalled worker): a ticket marked needs_human, never dispatched; LEDGER-004 records a raise failure at HIGH. The dedupe key LEDGER:<code>:<src> has no date (one standing card, never one per day); evidence is [code, src] only so a human refusal stays refused, and counts and recent messages go into the body; a closed ticket (updated_at, tickets.load_archived) or a withdrawn proposal (updated:) sets a floor so old entries never reopen it. Caps: 5 raises per fire, 3 new proposals per day (the first fire on that host had 22 candidates and stopped at the caps). Catalog rows live in design/specs/issue-codes.toml and are regenerated into docs/ISSUE-CODES.md by scripts/lib/issue_codes_gen.py (build_issue_codes.py); tests/test_ledger_to_card.py (15), test_issue_codes_spec.py (4), tests/test_scanner_walk_invariants.py. Defaults pending the owner, reversible: NO severity floor (asked 2026-11-19, unanswered; 93% of the ledger is LOW) and the dateless key.
