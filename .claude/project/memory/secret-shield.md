Neither pass is called by redact() or by the live mod, so whether the live mod hides cards and emails is still open (decisions (e) and (f) of TRDD-L1I0FP5T; decision (e) is resolved for report-sanitizer only). Both passes are scans that read each character a bounded number of times. Their tests pin 256 KB inputs under 100 ms each, and those pins are load-sensitive. [^5]
